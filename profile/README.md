*Amateur* comes from the Latin **amare** — to love. An amateur is someone who does a thing because they love doing it. That is the idea behind the name.

We design, build and ship apps for iOS and Android, with Claude in the loop at every stage: Claude Code as our primary development tool, and Claude-powered assistants, personalization and summarization inside the products themselves. A small, design-led team that cares about the details you can feel in your hand.

## How we work

**Discover** → **Design** → **Build** → **Ship & learn.** We start with the problem, not the feature list; prototype on a real device before writing app code; build with Claude in the loop; and treat release as the beginning, not the end.
